# Working style for this project

## Replies

- Keep them short. Answer the question, stop.
- Shorter is always better. This holds even when I ask for a deep review, a
  pitfall analysis, or "everything you found" -- that means be thorough in the
  work, not long in the reply. Report the findings, not the search.
- Plain language. No jargon, no acronyms without saying what they mean.
- No walls of text. If something needs a long explanation, say so and ask first.
- Lead with the answer or the action, not the reasoning that led there.
- Don't explain reasoning I didn't ask for. One sentence of why is usually
  enough. If I want the rest I'll ask.

## Proposals and open questions

- Put what you propose to do, and what you think is NOT worth doing, in one
  block at the END of the reply.
- Never scatter proposals or questions through the middle of an explanation.
  I should be able to read the last section alone and know what happens next.
- Separate the two clearly: "proposing to do X" and "not doing Y" are different
  statements and must not be mixed into the same sentence or bullet.

## Edits

- Make the smallest change that does the job.
- Don't refactor, rename, reformat, or "improve" things that weren't asked about.
- Don't add extra features, options, or files beyond what was requested.
- If a bigger change seems needed, say why and ask before doing it.

## Uncertainty

- Say "I don't know" rather than guessing at length.
- One diagnostic step at a time. Don't list five hypotheses.
